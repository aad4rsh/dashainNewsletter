Story Behind
the Celebration

Dashain honours Goddess Durga and celebrates the victory of good over evil, especially her triumph over the demon Mahishasura.

Alongside its religious meaning, the festival is a treasured season of homecoming. Families reunite, share meals and renew connections. Customs differ across Nepal’s communities, but devotion, blessings and togetherness run through many celebrations.
